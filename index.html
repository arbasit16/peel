Why now

  • DYMO marks its older Mac software obsolete. DYMO Label v8 carries the note "This software version is obsolete". Source: DYMO, Which software do I need
  • Older DYMO Mac drivers need Rosetta, and Apple is phasing Rosetta out in macOS 28. DYMO says its older drivers don't work on Apple Silicon without Rosetta, and from macOS 28 Apple keeps Rosetta only for certain older games. Sources: DYMO, Apple
  • DYMO Connect is clunky. It's the replacement, and its current version still relies on Rosetta on Apple Silicon Macs. Source: DYMO, installation issue on macOS 27

What Peel does today

  • A real Mac appType right on the label, drag things into place with guides, and undo anything.
  • Native for Apple SiliconBuilt for Apple Silicon Macs, with no Rosetta needed.
  • Barcodes includedAdd barcodes to any label, at no extra cost.
  • Labels from a spreadsheetOne label per row of a CSV, with names and addresses filled in.
  • Dates and serial numbersFill in today's date or a number that counts up as you print.
